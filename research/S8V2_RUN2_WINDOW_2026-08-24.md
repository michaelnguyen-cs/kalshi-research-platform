## Carried forward from the frozen rule

The disclosed limitation applies unchanged: the score counts only games
beginning inside the window, never games already in progress, because counting
those requires a game-duration assumption published schedules do not supply. The
selected window is anchored at the start of the evening's largest simultaneous
slate; whether that is weakly easier or harder than a mid-slate window is
recorded as undetermined, and was not a selection input.
